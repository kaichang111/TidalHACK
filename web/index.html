Fault Contribution Hierarchy

Evidence-based accident fault estimation

1 · Evidence 2 · Review facts 3 · Report

1 · Submit evidence

Upload photos, dashcam video and written accounts. Facts are extracted automatically — extraction never assigns fault. Or start from a sample case.

2 · Review case facts

A human reviewer confirms or corrects every fact before scoring. Leave unknown values blank — the engine will not guess them.
